# ADR 0002: Close SMAIP as a completed study

Status: accepted, 2026-11-13. Supersedes ADR 0001's open research-track commitment.

## Context

The owner chose to conclude the project at the existing evidence and present
it as a study of what was investigated and found. The original BRD's stronger
detector, agent-defense, integration, and operating-performance gates remain
unmet. Additional experiments are outside the chosen scope.

## Decision

Close SMAIP as a bounded research and engineering study. Present the research
questions, methods, measured outcomes, negative results, limitations, and final
interpretation in the README and study conclusion. Preserve original reports,
private reviews, disputed-label quarantine, models, and thresholds.

Retain the original checklist steps 9–13 as unperformed optional extensions.
Do not mark them passed or describe study closure as full BRD acceptance.
No new dataset acquisition, GPU experiment, product expansion, or deployment
is required for closure. Any later extension starts a separately scoped study.

## Consequences

The repository's purpose is to let readers inspect the work, run the scripted
guardrail demonstration, and learn from the experiments and their limitations.
The final deliverable is the documented study and experimental framework.
